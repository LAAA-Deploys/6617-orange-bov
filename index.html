Five closed sales of rent-stabilized buildings of 16 or more units, built between 1950 and 1969, recorded within 1.3 miles of the subject between May 2025 and August 2026. Every one of those buyers borrowed against cheaper money than a buyer does today. Thirty days before each sale the 3-year Treasury stood between 3.58% and 4.23% and the 5-year between 3.72% and 4.31%; on September 16, 2026 they were 4.82% and 4.86% (U.S. Treasury constant maturity, via FRED). Positioning Thesis

The right buyer values what is uncommon at this size: an elevator building in Beverly Grove with its seismic, balcony, roof and electrical work finished and documented, a Walk Score of 95, and occupied one-bedroom rents about 13% under the pro forma's $2,495 that move to market as tenants turn over. That buyer should be qualified early on two questions, whether they accept the underwritten insurance and repair figures or underwrite to the 2025 return, and whether they are financing at today's rates or buying with modest debt, because both decide where inside the range an offer lands.

Transaction Strategy

01

Lead With the Work Already Done

A buyer of a 1962 wood-frame building in Los Angeles asks two questions before any other: the soft-story retrofit and the balcony inspection. Both are closed here. The retrofit carries a Certificate of Compliance from March 2018 and the balcony repair in 20 units was finaled in April 2024, alongside a 2017 roof and building-wide electrical subpanels finaled in 2023. The package leads with those permits, because they remove the capital reserve a buyer would otherwise subtract from the price.

02

Put the Real Operating History in the Package

The 2025 return shows $55,066 of insurance and $50,062 of repairs, painting, appliances and supplies, both well above what this underwriting carries. A buyer will see both. The package states the underwritten figures and the booked figures side by side, with the reason for each, so a buyer underwrites once rather than finding the gap in diligence and re-trading. The insurance declarations page and a 2025 repair ledger are the two documents that make that case, and we recommend having both in hand before launch.

Show the Rent Gap on the Occupied Units

All 21 units are under the Rent Stabilization Ordinance.
